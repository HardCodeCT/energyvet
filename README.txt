ENERGY VETERINARY AND AGRO-ALLIED SERVICES - WEBSITE
=====================================================

FOLDER STRUCTURE:
  index.html          → Main website (open in any browser)
  logo.png            → Your brand logo
  images/             → Drop all your images here
  images/IMAGES-README.txt → Full list of image filenames

HOW TO ADD YOUR IMAGES:
  1. Put your photos in the images/ folder
  2. Name them exactly as listed in images/IMAGES-README.txt
  3. Open index.html — images load automatically

CUSTOMIZE TEXT:
  Open index.html in any text editor and search/replace:
  - "+234 000 0000"     → your phone number
  - "info@energyvet.com" → your email
  - "Aba, Abia State"   → your address
