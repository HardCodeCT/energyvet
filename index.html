Help center

Frequently asked questions

Do you treat both pets and farm animals? ▾
Yes — Energy Veterinary and Agro-Allied Services caters to household pets, livestock, poultry, and exotic animals. Our team includes specialists in both small and large animal care, as well as agro-allied advisory services for farms of all sizes.
Can I book a same-day appointment? ▾
Yes, same-day and emergency appointments are available depending on clinic capacity. You can call us directly or visit to check availability. Walk-ins are also welcome when time permits.
What vaccinations do you offer? ▾
We offer core and non-core vaccines for dogs, cats, cattle, goats, poultry, and more. Our veterinarians will advise on the most appropriate vaccination schedule based on your animal's species, age, and environment.
Do you provide on-farm visit services? ▾
Yes, our agro-allied team provides on-farm visits for herd health checks, farm advisory, disease outbreak response, and livestock management consultations. Contact us to schedule a farm visit at a time that works for you.
Do you have a pharmacy on-site? ▾
Yes, we stock a range of veterinary medications, dewormers, supplements, and agro-allied inputs. Our team will recommend the right products for your animals' needs after examination.
